# Dramatic Action Brief — *The Claim Against the Path*
## Issue 71 · Season 1, Episode 2, Act 1, Scene 1
## Phase 7 — Yesod

---

### Overview

Eight sequential beats. This scene opens Episode 2 and constitutes the Wolf's fourth attempt. Its mechanism — a topographical claim that borrows authority from prior verified information, tested against what Red's body already knows of the route — operates across five structural beats (B002 through B006), bracketed by the opening terrain exchange (B001) and the scene's closing surface (B007–B008). The drama does not live in confrontation; it lives in the quality of the mismatch, the form of the probe, the specific cost of the recovery, and the gap between what Red has filed and what the Wolf believes it has neutralized.

The beats are dependent in order. Each beat rests on the state the previous one established. Character choices, dialogue, and specific lines remain open.

---

### Shared Facts at Scene Entry

- Red enters Episode 2 carrying the residue of Episode 1's four scenes. The basket is in her left hand. The bread is cool — the warmth from the gate has been gone since the close of Episode 1. The jar beneath the cloth is heavier, cooler, unchanged.
- The wet right shoe: present since Act 1, Scene 1, Beat 3 of Episode 1 (issue-67). Neither resolved nor mentioned since.
- The ford is behind Red. She crossed it before the second bend in the path. The near bank was firm — no give, ordinary dry-bank approach. One careful step from the bank onto the first crossing-stone, her hand going briefly to the basket handle before settling back. The crossing took no special management.
- The second bend is also behind her. Both bends are past. The path she is on now runs in the section after the canopy has fully thinned — softer earth underfoot, more open acoustic, sky available in patches.
- Red carries three private inventory items from Episode 1, none voiced: (1) the birchwood inconsistency — the Wolf named the turning for the far houses before Red disclosed her destination, evidentiary and testable; (2) the endearment-and-cooling sequence — the *honey* mid-offer, the warmth returning shorter after her refusal, atmospheric and difficult to make legible outside her own register; (3) the extension at the close of Episode 1, Scene 4 — the Wolf placed itself in the journey's continuation without asking permission, relational and unanswered.
- The Wolf's directions and observations in Episode 1 all proved accurate: the dry ridge, the root-crossing sequence, the birchwood fork, the softer earth past the old growth. Its practical credibility is intact and confirmed by Red's body. The claim to follow will borrow that credibility by arriving in the same voice and register.
- The Wolf enters Episode 2 operating on a misreading: it filed Red's non-answer to the extension (Episode 1, Scene 4, Beat 6) as tacit acceptance. Its confidence in its own expertise-claim is intact. It has not yet had that claim tested.
- The terrain underfoot: the yielding quality the Wolf described has arrived exactly as predicted. She recognized it approaching and felt it land in her body before she could name it. This is fresh confirmation of its accuracy — the most recent of the verified facts.
