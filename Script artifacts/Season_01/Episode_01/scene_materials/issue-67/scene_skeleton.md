# Scene Skeleton — *Red Sets Out*
**Issue:** 67  
**Episode:** Season 1, Episode 1  
**Act:** 1  
**Phase:** 6 — Hod

## Beat: Departure and First Private Beat
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-67-B001] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. GARDEN GATE / OPEN PATH — EARLY MORNING

LIGHTING: Low autumn sun, working sideways. The open stretch near the gate receives the light clean and direct — no canopy interference, the day still new enough that the ground holds its early chill. The path ahead is legible in both directions: behind, the familiar gate; ahead, the birch stand waiting at the tree-line.

AUDIO: The latch click as the gate settles behind her — a particular sound, reliable, the same as always. Morning birdsong without urgency. Packed earth underfoot, the occasional scatter of fallen leaf. The basket does not rattle.

CAMERA: RED in motion already, gate at her back. She is not pausing at the threshold; the departure is a continuation, not a performance. The basket is in her left hand. The red hood settles on her shoulders.

ACTION: [RED walks the open stretch. The basket is already speaking to her hands — the bread's warmth through the wrapping, and beneath that, the cooler, distinct weight of the jar. She reads both without looking. The information is tactile before it becomes thought. She is moving well. The clock is in the warmth; she is not watching it yet.]

PARENTHETICAL: (private, unhurried — a thought arriving before she has reached for it)

DIALOGUE: [RED — to herself — the private pleasure of the jar surfaces: the knowledge that she chose it, that it was her idea, that grandmother will know it was her idea. The warmth and the specificity of the cooler weight below arrive together. This is her private pleasure, more precise than satisfaction with the errand in general.]

ACTION: [She stops briefly — one step, not a full halt. She adjusts the cloth over the basket's contents: confirms the jar has not shifted in transit, confirms the bread's warmth is still there under her fingers. The check is unhurried. There is no one watching. The care is complete without an audience to receive it. She resettles the cloth and keeps walking.]

PARENTHETICAL: (smaller, inward — the thought that contains grandmother as a face, not a destination)

DIALOGUE: [RED — private — the specific person she is going toward: a preference she knows well enough to have gotten right. Not duty. Pleasure that arrived from knowledge. She chose the right thing. She knows she chose the right thing. Grandmother will know.]

AUDIO: Her footsteps lengthening slightly as the birch stand approaches. The gate sound is already behind and finished.

TRANSITION: She enters the birch stand and the light shifts.

## Beat: The Woods Receiving Her Attention
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-67-B002] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. BIRCH STAND — EARLY MORNING

LIGHTING: Strip pattern — vertical bars of morning light alternating with filtered grey-green shadow. Red reads this light easily, navigates without thinking about navigating. The patterns are familiar configurations; her body is ahead of her attention on the known landmarks.

AUDIO: The birdsong continues, now wrapped in the birch ambient — a slightly different acoustic from the open path. The basket is still, contents secure. Her footfall changes quality as the ground shifts from packed earth to the softer floor of the wood's edge. 

CAMERA: RED moving through the strips of light and shadow. She is interested. Her head turns toward something specific — a root configuration, a quality of light on bark, the texture of the path where it goes briefly soft and then firms again. The curiosity is real and physical; it is not alertness organized by caution.

PARENTHETICAL: (quick, private, more candid than her spoken voice would allow before a listener — an observation that reaches the edge of a formed opinion and arrives at itself without editing)

DIALOGUE: [RED — to herself — noticing something particular in the morning: a quality of light on a specific root, or the way the bark reads at this angle. Her private voice runs faster here than her courtesy would permit; the opinion is sharper and more particular than she would offer aloud. She may catch the thought before it finishes forming and redirect — a self-correction at the edge of a judgment, briefly funnier for being only hers.]

ACTION: [She shifts the basket from left hand to right — an unremarkable migration, the weight redistributing across her grip. She does not comment on it. Her attention is already ahead: the point where the packed path first goes soft, the step left of the known mud pool, the fallen beech she can see at the edge of her peripheral range. These come to her before she reaches them, from memory living in her feet.]

PARENTHETICAL: (a fragment — an observation that begins with a concrete detail and doesn't need to finish)

DIALOGUE: [RED — a second private note, briefer than the first — the kind of candid attention that has nowhere to hide when there is no one watching. Particular, with an opinion embedded in it. Not performing ease; occupying it.]

LIGHTING: The birch stands begin giving way to older trees. The strip pattern of the light will change here — taller trunks, the strips going longer and beginning to work horizontally. The quality shifts: the light in the older trees carries a different weight. Still beautiful; slightly more interior.

AUDIO: Upper branches in slight movement — a slow, irregular sound from overhead that is different from the birdsong. The ambient under the older trees sits closer to silence. Not threatening: attending.

CAMERA: RED noticing the shift in light quality without naming it. Her private voice does not comment on the change directly — but her pace is unchanged, and she is still interested.

## Beat: The Mud-Shoe Event
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-67-B003] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. WOODLAND PATH — OLDER TREES — EARLY MORNING

CAMERA: The path edge — an exposed root catching the low horizontal light, not at the centre of attention. Water held just under the surface at the verge. RED moving with her attention ahead and partially inward, the basket now in her right hand, the path familiar enough that this section does not ask for focused navigation.

ACTION: [The physical fact arrives without announcement: the heel seam — a line she trusted — gives way under cold water finding its path through the failure. Her foot registers it before she does. She catches her balance against the lurch; the basket swings in the correction, her grip tightening on it. The jar holds. The bread holds.]

AUDIO: The brief scuff of the lurch — her footing recovered, the path continuing. Then silence in the spot where the quip would go if it were going anywhere.

PARENTHETICAL: (the gap — before composure arrives — just the sensation: cold, specific, wrong)

DIALOGUE: [RED — the gap itself, which is not a line: the unvarnished physical fact of wet, arriving before she has assembled anything around it. This beat is the absence of a response, not a response. It lasts only a moment.]

PARENTHETICAL: (dry, fast, for no one — the voice finding its form on the other side of the physical fact)

DIALOGUE: [RED — to herself — the ironic phrase that makes the small indignity bearable without requiring a witness. Brief, slightly funnier than anything she would say to a person. The canon supplies the form: dry understatement, ironic compression, the private humor of someone who does not need to perform recovery for an audience. The specific line is the writing phase's to find; it must follow the sensation, not anticipate it.]

ACTION: [She recovers quickly: resets her footing, adjusts the basket back to her preferred grip, and keeps walking. The wet shoe travels forward with her, unremarked upon again. Her pace does not break. She does not revisit the moment.]

CAMERA: RED continuing down the path, the wet-shoe step absorbed into her stride. The path ahead is ordinary; the light in the older trees continues horizontal and quiet.

## Beat: The Fork
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-67-B004] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. PATH FORK — OLDER TREES — EARLY MORNING

AUDIO: The near-silence of the older trees. No new sound; the ambient continues as it was. It is quiet enough that she notices the quality of the quiet.

CAMERA: RED arriving at the fork. Two paths, both showing wear, both plausible. She stops — not a full stop, but her stride contracts. She looks at both options.

LIGHTING: Cloud cover has shifted — the low sun is less cooperative at this moment. The horizontal light that might give a reading on the root patterns at the fork's mouth is not offering a clean direction. The left path's mud shows churning at the edge; the evidence is thin and genuine.

ACTION: [The fork has arrived earlier than her mental map placed it. She knows roughly where she is; she knows the fork must be somewhere ahead; it is here before she expected it. She looks at the two paths. The left path's mud churning could mean foot traffic or animal passage — neither interpretation resolves to a direction. Her direction-sense reads left. The sun is not confirming.]

PARENTHETICAL: (quieter than Beat 2 — engaged with the actual navigational problem, not performing ease)

DIALOGUE: [RED — private — her direction-sense reading aloud to herself. Brief, practical, specific: left is what she has. The available evidence and its thinness. She does not argue with herself at length; she registers what she has and what she does not.]

ACTION: [The bodily decision arrives ahead of the deliberation settling it: her stride lengthens into the left path, the basket migrating across her hands in the reset of her grip, her weight moving forward. She is walking the left path before the argument is fully closed. The provisional quality of the commitment is filed without ceremony — she does not stage the doubt and does not perform confidence. The path is underfoot.]

PARENTHETICAL: (after the committed stride — the private register recovering slightly, quieter than Beat 2 but no longer contracted by the navigational problem)

DIALOGUE: [RED — to herself — the brief private note that follows the committed stride: that this counts, or that she'll find out, or that the evidence was what it was and she used it. Shorter than a verdict. Not reassurance. Something closer to filing the question without needing to close it.]

CAMERA: RED continuing down the left path. The fork is behind her. Her pace is consistent; the basket is in her hands. The question is filed.

AUDIO: Her footsteps on the left path, the birdsong ambient, the slight movement of upper branches. Ordinary morning woodland sounds.

## Beat: The Sound and the Walk On
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-67-B005] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. WOODLAND PATH — OLDER TREES — LEFT FORK — EARLY MORNING

ACTION: [RED walking the left path, the fork behind her. The older trees continue; the horizontal light continues. She is in her own momentum.]

AUDIO: The near-silence of the older trees: birdsong not absent but not close, the ambient sitting just short of quiet. Then — from behind her, not from beside or above — a sound that does not belong to the ambient register. It has weight: the specific character of displacement, something with mass redistributing at the edge of hearing. A branch settling under sustained pressure. An interval that is wrong. Not loud; just wrong in its quality.

CAMERA: RED's stride slowing — then stopping. She turns.

ACTION: [She turns. She looks at the path behind her, the trees, the gap between trunks. There is nothing to see. The path she has come from is ordinary. She looks long enough to be certain she is looking, not long enough to be waiting for something to resolve.]

LIGHTING: The ordinary morning light on the path behind her. Nothing disturbed in the visible frame.

AUDIO: The ambient returns to its previous quality. The sound does not repeat. The near-silence settles.

PARENTHETICAL: (not the gap where a quip would go — this is the absence of the quip, which is different)

DIALOGUE: [RED — nothing. The space where a dry remark would normally arrive and make the moment bearable does not produce one. This is not performed silence; it is the specific quality of a moment that does not close neatly enough for private irony. The humor is absent because the question does not resolve — not because she is frightened into stillness. The absence itself is the legible register shift.]

ACTION: [She keeps walking. The stride that resumes is not different in pace; it is different in what it carries. She does not promise herself the sound was nothing. She makes a different promise: that it is not going to slow her down. Both things are present in the way she holds herself as she goes — the woods are the kind of place where the first promise is not available to her, and she knows it, and she walks anyway.]

CAMERA: RED moving forward down the left path, the older trees receiving her, the scene's last image her back and the basket in her hand and the path ahead and the unresolved sound behind that she has declined to let stop her.

AUDIO: Her footsteps continuing. The ambient. The silence where the quip would go traveling forward with her.

TRANSITION: She is inside her own momentum. The scene ends.
