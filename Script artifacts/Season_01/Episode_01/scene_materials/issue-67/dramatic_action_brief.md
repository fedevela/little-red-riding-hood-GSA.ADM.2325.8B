## Continuity Out

Red enters Scene 2 (*The First Meeting*) carrying:

**A provisional navigational commitment to the left fork.** Her best available decision on thin evidence — not a mistake she is unaware of, not settled confidence. The Wolf's navigational advice will land into a real uncertainty, not a manufactured one.

**An unresolved sound she turned toward, could not explain, and has declined to let slow her.** She is in the kind of place where she cannot promise herself that every sound is nothing. She has only the second promise: that this one is not going to stop her.

**The basket, still warm.** The bread's warmth is finite; she is moving. The season's measure is in her hands before the encounter begins to spend it.
