## Beat: The Wolf's Private Filing

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. OLDER WOOD — THE WOLF'S POSITION — CONTINUOUS

ACTION: The Wolf holds its position as the scene reaches its closing interval. Its private accounting does not appear in its posture as legible satisfaction — just the ease of a creature that has remained where it was, watching her carry the scene forward without having named what it placed. Its private reckoning files the warmth she accepted as more permission than she granted. Her non-answer reads as tacit acceptance. The Scene 3 confirmation (she can separate offer from terms) and this scene's warmth are filed together: the approach of accurate observation is effective; the relationship can absorb a refusal and recover.
