## Playable frame

Red enters after the marked creek crossing with the covered basket, provisions,
destination, and route judgment intact. The three creek marks and angled branch
remain behind her, as do the earlier two-twig mark and blocked road. The trace
is wet but visibly open; late-day light is thinning. The Wolf may remain several
paces away, offer information, and later withdraw ease or guidance, but it may
not block, touch, seize the basket, or make this passage materially impossible.

The scene distinguishes useful help from consent to closeness. The Wolf's
attention may feel briefly affirming, then become conditional on company or
reach. Red's boundary remains effective while its social cost becomes visible.
No private motive is shared between the characters, no response is fixed, and
the final trap and all later outcomes remain unresolved. Mother and grandmother
remain offstage.
