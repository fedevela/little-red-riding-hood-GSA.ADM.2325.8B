## Beat: Arrival Into Changed Ground

SCENE_HEADING: The older-tree section, further down the left path. The canopy is higher and the trees stand further apart than the birch stand. Light arrives in pools of direct sun interrupted by deeper shade — bright intervals, then closed shadow, then bright again. The path's centre-spine is packed firm; a soft depression collects standing water along the right edge. An elevated ridge of dry packed earth runs close to the left edge. The acoustic is more open here than the birch stand; sound carries above the wider canopy spacing.

TRANSITION: Red enters from the older-tree shade, her pace carrying the stride that preceded the deliberation at the fork — the left path and its provisional quality still in her body. The wet shoe is a background fact. The basket is in her left hand; the bread is still warm through the wrapping.

LIGHTING: The path shifts from the horizontal planes of the older-tree section to a brighter, more intermittent quality. The first pool of direct sun catches the elevated dry edge along the left side of the path. The Wolf's dark coat is present in or near that brighter stretch.

AUDIO: The ambient sound register is thinner here than it was in the birch stand. Birdsong exists at distance, in the upper canopy; it is not close. The general quiet sits differently from Scene 1's near-silence — more open, more space for a voice to arrive clearly into.

ACTION: Red comes through from the shade into the first open pool of sun. Her feet register the transition from soft forest floor to the firmer centre-spine of the path; the packed earth is a physical fact before it is a noticed fact. She continues forward. The elevated dry edge and the soft depression along the right are both visible now. The wet shoe goes forward with her without ceremony.

## Beat: The Wolf Already There
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B002] -->

SCENE_HEADING: The elevated dry-edge section of the path, where the packed earth rises slightly above the soft ground to the right. Pools of direct sun at intervals. The Wolf is present near the dry edge in the way that a creature at ease in this section of wood is present there — not approaching, not performing arrival, simply occupying the position.

CAMERA: On the Wolf from Red's forward approach. Its dark coat is distinct against the lighter pool of sun. Its head is angled toward the terrain — the listening tilt — not turned toward the approaching path. Its weight is distributed and still, comfortable, in no hurry.

LIGHTING: The Wolf stands near the brighter stretch. Its dark fur absorbs the light; the surrounding open sun makes it visible without making it dramatic. It is not concealed. It is not displayed. It is simply present where the footing is good.

ACTION: The Wolf holds its position near the dry edge. It does not turn toward Red's footsteps. It does not perform a greeting. It is already there; Red arrives into a figure that has been standing still for some time in a place where the footing is good. The bodily strength is present in the patience of the stillness — weight settled, watchful eyes available but not forward.

PARENTHETICAL: The Wolf's presence in this stretch is not an arrival. The scene begins with it already occupying this position. Its first line arises from where it stands.

## Beat: Red Tests Before She Follows
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B004] -->

ACTION: Red slows slightly — not stopping, not halting, but a small reduction in pace that belongs to someone who receives advice by checking it before following. She steps left toward the elevated dry edge. The test step is careful: the ball of the foot first, the weight shifting only after the ground has responded. The elevated ridge proves solid and dry under the foot.

PARENTHETICAL: This is not visible suspicion. It is the ordinary attentiveness of someone who notices things. The Wolf sees competence checking a claim.

ACTION: She commits her weight to the dry edge and continues forward along it. Her pace resumes. The advice was accurate; the exchange is now on practical terms. The basket is in her left hand throughout.

CAMERA: On Red's feet — the careful first step, the weight transfer, the resumed forward motion. Brief. The test is readable in her body without being readable as distrust.

AUDIO: The sound of her footstep on the packed dry earth is distinctly different from the soft depression it is avoiding. The difference is physical and immediate.

## Beat: The Grip That Does Not Complete
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B006] -->

ACTION: Red is walking, her weight going forward, the basket in her left hand. The useful sentence arrives at her ears as continuous — she is receiving it while still moving. The practical guidance comes through clearly. Then, inside the flow of the sentence, the wrong word arrives. Her hands are on the basket at the moment it passes.

PARENTHETICAL: What follows is not a gesture of checking (Beat 1 in Scene 1: the care-grip). It is not a gesture of recovery (Scene 1 Beat 3: the post-lurch press). It is not the hold-grip of Scene 1's Beat 5 (not checking, just holding). This is the fourth quality: arrested-attention, incomplete. A hand adjusting before the mind has assembled the cause.

ACTION: The grip on the basket handle adjusts. The adjustment is fractional — a slight increase in contact, the kind of shift that happens when a small intake of attention travels to the nearest physical point. It is brief. It is incomplete. It does not become a purposeful check. Her face does not change. Her weight is still going forward.

CAMERA: On Red's hands, briefly — the grip shift and its arrest. Then back to her forward motion. The adjustment reads from the Wolf's vantage point as the ordinary management of weight in a hand that has been carrying something for some distance.

PARENTHETICAL: The Wolf's attention is on Red's face and her forward motion. From its angle of observation, the grip shift does not separate from ordinary weight management. The audience, watching the hands, can read it as noticing.

ACTION: She does not turn toward the Wolf. She does not ask a question. She keeps walking.

## Beat: The Silence Where the Quip Usually Lives
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B007] -->

PARENTHETICAL: The quip pattern has been established twice in Scene 1 — the wet shoe and the fork's ambiguity both generated private ironic recovery. Scene 1's Beat 5 (the unresolved sound) broke the pattern once already: the scene closed in held grip and forward motion, no verbal recovery. That was an environmental and open occasion. This is a social and particular one.

ACTION: Red walks forward. The sentence is complete. The Wolf's guidance sits in her ear alongside the wrong word. She has received both at once and cannot extract one without the other.

PARENTHETICAL: A small difficulty — a word she has received that does not fit — would normally, in her private register, find form. Brief, dry, candid, for no one. The kind of closing that makes a mismatch bearable by holding it at a small ironic distance. The subject for that form has not resolved into something she can name yet. The word is still working in her. Irony requires a subject that has closed enough to be turned. This has not closed.

ACTION: She continues walking. The next thing that happens is her forward motion. The absence of the closing phrase lives inside the continuity of that motion, not in a pause or a change of rhythm.

AUDIO: No change in her footfalls. No held breath. No shift in the ambient. The silence is not staged; it lives in what does not arrive.

## Beat: What the Wolf Files
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B008] -->

PARENTHETICAL: The Wolf's attention has been on Red's face and forward motion throughout the exchange. Its confidence in this meeting is genuine — it is at ease, the advice it offered was accurate, the exchange proceeded on practical terms. The slip in the useful sentence was the cost of ease: a sentence constructed from comfort and habit, not from careful tracking of what Red had and had not disclosed.

ACTION: The Wolf observes the quality of Red's forward motion as she continues past. Something in it — a fractional caught quality in a person who has been moving fluidly — may register in the Wolf's private attention.

PARENTHETICAL: The Wolf cannot be certain what she attended to. The grip shift reads as ordinary weight management from its position. The observation is not legible as suspicion. But the private acknowledgment — the small, unflustered recognition that she caught something — is available to the Wolf's internal register. It does not respond. Responding would expose the slip. The recognition is filed without urgency; its confidence at this stage means the filing carries no alarm.

PARENTHETICAL: The self-excusing mechanism may be present here in the Wolf's private register: the observation framed as her being sharp, or the basket being heavy, or the path being long. The exact form belongs to the writing phase. What must be present is that the acknowledgment is private, genuine, and unflustered — not a performance of calm but an actual calm that will require revision later as the Wolf's certainty about what Red noticed begins to grow uncertain.

ACTION: The Wolf remains where it is near the dry edge. It does not call after Red. It does not move to follow. It watches her forward motion until she is further down the path.

## Beat: She Is Carrying Both
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-68-B009] -->

SCENE_HEADING: The path continuing beyond the dry-edge section. The older trees on either side. Red walking forward, further down the left path, the Wolf's position behind her.

LIGHTING: The pools of direct sun and shadow continue along the path ahead. The brighter stretch near the dry edge recedes behind her.

ACTION: Red walks forward with both items now in her — the unresolved sound from Scene 1 and the birchwood word from this exchange. These are distinct in kind. The sound is environmental and open: something behind her on the path she has not been able to name, that might be anything. The word is social and particular: a name in someone else's mouth for a detail she knows she did not give. Neither has assembled into a conclusion. Both are present.

PARENTHETICAL: She has the word. She does not yet have the sentence that makes the word impossible. The evidentiary chain is present in her knowledge — grandmother is on the birchwood side; she did not say so; the Wolf named it correctly — but the chain has not yet connected into an interpretation she can name. She is filing the mismatch, not its meaning. Doubt is arriving first as a mismatch.

ACTION: The basket is in her left hand. The bread is warm through the cloth. The errand continues. She is still on the left path, which is the path the Wolf's guidance presupposed as correct. The navigation and the inconsistency arrived in the same sentence; she cannot use the slip to discard the direction.

CAMERA: On Red walking forward, from behind and slightly above. The left path receiving her footsteps. The basket in her left hand, the warmth of the bread against the cloth. The older trees continuing around her on either side.

AUDIO: Her footfalls on the path. The ambient at its open, thin register — birdsong distant, upper-canopy movement occasional, the general quiet of this section of wood. The Wolf's voice is gone; it belongs behind her now, filed with the dry-edge section.

PARENTHETICAL: The act ends here. Neither item is an alarm. Both are present. The quip that would have made the birchwood word manageable did not come. Red is further down the path with the warmth of the bread still real in her hands and a pebble she has not yet named in her ear.
