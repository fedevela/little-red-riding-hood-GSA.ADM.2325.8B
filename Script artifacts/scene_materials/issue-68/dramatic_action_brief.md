## Director's Notes

This is the encounter's first frame. Two characters now occupy the same scene: the Wolf and Red. The Wolf has been standing near the dry-edge footing in this section of path before Red arrives; it is the scene's established condition, not its event. The scene distributes its nine dramatic moments across a single continuous passage: Red entering changed ground, seeing the Wolf already present, receiving its practical first line, testing and following its advice, receiving the sentence that contains the slip, the grip shift that does not complete, the silence where a quip would normally go, the Wolf privately filing what it observed, and Red continuing down the path carrying both unresolved items.

No prior episode has been performed before Scene 1 (issue-67). Scene 1 is now performed and its text is preserved in the immutable snapshot at `Script artifacts/scene_materials/issue-68/prior_performance.md`. Everything Red carries into this scene is drawn from that performed text. The Wolf's knowledge of Red's destination precedes this scene's frame; it cannot have been established through this encounter.

Two characters are present across this scene. Red perceives and acts in Beats 1 through 9; the Wolf perceives and acts in Beats 2, 3, 5, and 8. Beat 8 is the Wolf's only moment with a private register developed in the brief's character block; the other Wolf beats develop its physical presence and speech stimuli available to Red.

The basket is always in Red's hands. The wrong word, once spoken, is permanently in the scene whether or not it is named. Neither character breaks the ordinariness of the exchange in any external register.
