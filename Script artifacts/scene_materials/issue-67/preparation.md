# Phase 5 — Netzach: Preparation
## *Red Sets Out*

**Issue:** 67
**Episode:** Script/Season_01/Episode_01
**Act:** 1

---

## Placement and Continuity

This is the opening scene of Season 1, Episode 1, Act 1. No performed scenes precede it;
no continuity notes exist to carry forward. The season's dramatic record begins here.
The Wolf has not yet entered. Red is entirely alone.

This preparation supersedes the earlier iteration from issue-29. The dramatic intention
has been refined and specified through the Tiferet synthesis: five confirmed beats with
distinct sequencing requirements, a settled basket inventory (bread and preserved plum jar),
and the particular two-dispositions distinction at the close. Those specifics govern
the preparation below.

## Environmental Conditions

### Light

Early autumn or late-winter morning — the season consistent with grandmother preferring
preserved plum, a flavor she returns to in autumn and early winter. The sun is low and
working sideways through the canopy rather than overhead. On the open stretch near the
gate, the light is clean and direct. Once Red enters the birch stand it angles and splits:
vertical strips of brightness alternating with filtered grey-green shadow. She reads this
light easily — it is familiar territory, recognizable patterns.

The transition into older trees changes the quality. The strips go horizontal; the light
comes in long, flat planes that catch dust and carry a different weight. It is beautiful
here in the way that is also slightly quieting. The private voice that runs easily in the
birch stands may go a degree more interior in the older trees.

At the fork, a shift in cloud cover changes the light reading on the path. The roots and
turning markers that might give compass guidance are not well-lit in this moment. The
unreliability is physical, not imagined. This is when the navigational evidence becomes
genuinely thin.

### Sound

Morning woodland: birdsong without urgency. Upper branches in slight movement. Irregular
footfall percussion — packed earth alternating with sections that have gone soft with
ground moisture. The basket does not rattle: the jar is wrapped and held still by the
cloth; the bread is swaddled. Red's passage is quieter than she probably assumes.

The ambient sits just short of silence — the kind of quiet that can be read as peaceful
or as attending. It is not threatening until the final beat, and even then it is not
threatening. It is unresolved.

The sound that arrives in Beat 5 reaches into this near-silence. Its weight comes
precisely because the ambient is not loud. What warrants turning is not loudness —
it is displacement: the wrong quality of presence at the wrong interval. A branch
settling under pressure. A mass redistributing. The specific character of the sound
belongs to the writing phase; what must be present is that it is concrete enough to
warrant the turn and ordinary enough to remain genuinely unexplained.

### Setting and Path

The path originates at a garden gate — practical, reliable, its latch making a particular
sound when it catches. From the gate: a short open stretch, then the birch stand. Red
knows the familiar landmarks without thinking about knowing them: the fallen beech, the
step left of a known mud pool, the point where the packed path first goes soft. These she
navigates from memory. They are in her feet before they reach her attention.

The fork arrives earlier than her mental map placed it. Two paths, both showing wear, both
plausible. The distinction at the margin is ambiguous: the left path's mud shows more
churning at the edge — foot traffic or animal passage, not clearly one or the other. The
sun is not offering useful direction in this moment. The evidence is thin and genuine.

Twenty or so steps past the fork, and to one side: ground gone soft, water held just
under the surface. The path edge where someone would step to avoid it carries an exposed
root. This is the mud-shoe event's physical location — specific, unremarkable, the kind
of feature that is simply there and does not signal itself in advance.

Further past the stumble: the older trees, the horizontal light, the path quieter
underfoot. This is where the private thought can spread and range. It is also the
place from which she could not easily know if something had entered the path behind her.

### Physical Conditions and Props

**The basket.** Carried in the left hand primarily, switched to the right under load.
It is genuinely heavy: bread plus a corked jar of preserved plum. The bread's warmth
is tactile through the wrapping — a specific thermal fact, not decorative. The jar
sits cooler against it: a different weight, a different temperature.

When Red checks the basket in Beat 1 — adjusting the cloth, confirming the bread's
warmth — she is also confirming the jar has not shifted. The cooler weight is a
sensory assurance. The jar does not move; the bread is still warm. Both readings are
present in one gesture. The jar is never named aloud. It lives in her hands and her
private knowledge; it does not reach speech.

The basket's weight migrates across the scene: left hand, right hand, arm, forearm.
That migration is audible at the stumble (the shift of weight in a lurch) and at the
fork (the reset of the grip as she lengthens her stride). The basket must be present
wherever time is being spent.

**The red hood.** On her shoulders, belonging to her the way familiar things do —
not a symbol she is conscious of carrying, but part of what it feels like to be
herself. She is not performing it.

**The shoes.** Sturdy for woodland use. By the stumble, the heel seam of one shoe
has failed at a wet edge: cold water finding its path past a line she trusted. The
seam is not a dramatic failure; it is the ordinary unreliability of a material under
pressure it was not designed for, arriving at the exact wrong moment.

## The Five Beats — Sensory and Dramatic Register

### Beat 1: Departure and First Private Beat

Red is already in motion. The gate is behind her; the path is ahead; the familiar
open stretch is under her feet. The basket's warmth is already in her hands and she
is already reading it: bread warm through the wrapping, jar cooler beneath. Both
readings are present before she has gone far enough to worry about time — she knows
she is moving well.

The first private thought that surfaces is the jar. She chose it. Her mother did not
tell her to bring it; she knew grandmother's preference and made the selection herself.
Grandmother will know she chose it. There is a small, specific pleasure in being the
person who knew to bring the right thing — not the pleasure of performing care, but
the pleasure of care that lands precisely because it came from knowledge.

She checks the basket: adjusts the cloth, confirms the jar has not shifted, confirms
the bread is still warm. This is the scene's first visible act, and it has no audience.
The care is for its own sake. This is the episode's first image of her hidden wish to
be trusted — expressed through her hands, before speech.

*Register:* Private ease. Pleasure before any difficulty has organized it. The voice
here is the most relaxed it will be in this scene. Morning, purpose, warmth in the
hands, a small private satisfaction. She is not performing competence. She is simply
moving.

### Beat 2: The Woods Receiving Her Attention

Inside the trees now. The light shifts to its strip pattern; the roots come up in
configurations she recognizes. She is interested before she is cautious — the morning
is genuinely worth attending to. Her private voice runs in this register: quick,
concrete, more candid than courtesy would allow if someone were watching.

She notices things. An opinion about the quality of the light on a particular root.
A self-correction that reaches the edge of a thought and redirects before finishing.
A comparison that is more specific than it needs to be and slightly funnier for being
only hers. The mood is ease that is alive — not relaxation, but a specific attentiveness
that has nowhere to hide and doesn't need to.

The basket's weight is in her hands. She may switch it from left to right here,
unremarkably. The path texture shifts underfoot as the birch gives way to softer ground
between the older trees. She is noticing this, not narrating it.

*Register:* The private voice in its most relaxed and expansive form — before difficulty
compresses it. This is the voice the scene must establish clearly before Beat 3,
because Beat 3 and Beat 4 will test its structure. The audience needs to know what it
sounds like unchallenged so that its absence or compression in the later beats reads
as a register shift.

### Beat 3: The Mud-Shoe Event

The physical fact arrives first. The heel seam — a line she trusted — gives way:
cold water finding its path through the failure before she has processed anything.
There is a beat of just the sensation. Just wet, specific and cold, in the gap between
the event and the recovery.

Then her voice finds the form. Something dry and fast, for no one in particular:
the exact shape of ironic understatement that makes a small indignity bearable
without requiring a witness. The voice demonstration in her canon offers the form
(*Love that for me*) without prescribing the line — the line belongs to the writing
phase. What must be preserved is the gap: unvarnished physical sensation first,
irony second. If the humor precedes the stumble, it is performance. If it follows
the physical fact, it is the specific voice of someone making something bearable
in private.

She recovers quickly. The basket shifts in the lurch — she catches it, adjusts her
grip, and keeps walking. The wet shoe travels forward with her, unremarked upon
again.

*Register:* The humor is funnier for having no audience. It is sharper, more candid,
more particular than anything she would say to a person. The quip establishes the
scene's pattern: private difficulty followed by private recovery with ironic language.
This pattern is what Beat 5 will interrupt.

### Beat 4: The Fork

It arrives earlier than her mental map placed it. She expected to know where she was
until the fork; the fork is here before she was ready for it.

The markings are ambiguous. The light is not cooperative. The left path's mud shows
more churning at the edge, which could mean foot traffic or animals — neither
interpretation settles the question. Her direction-sense reads left; the available
evidence does not contradict that reading but does not confirm it either. She has
what she has.

Her best available action is to commit before the deliberation is complete. The
bodily decision arrives ahead of certainty: the stride lengthens, the basket shifts
across her hands, her weight moves forward. She is walking the left path before
the argument is settled. The provisional quality of the commitment is filed without
ceremony — she does not stage the doubt, she does not perform confidence. She just
moves.

Her private voice may go quieter here than it was at Beat 2 — she is genuinely
engaged with the navigational problem rather than at ease with it. After the stride
has committed, once the left path is underfoot and the question has been filed,
her register may recover: a private note that this counts, or doesn't, or will
be findable-out soon enough.

*Register:* The forward motion is the scene's fourth beat of Red's capacity — the
quality the scene is displaying rather than teaching. The uncommitted commitment
is not a flaw she needs to correct. It is the form of forward motion available to
someone whose terrain knowledge has edges. She arrives at what follows holding a
provisional commitment, not a settled certainty.

### Beat 5: The Sound and the Walk On

Before she moves away from the fork: a sound arrives behind her.

It has weight — not the birdsong ambient, not the ordinary creak of upper branches.
It belongs to the category of sounds that warrant turning: a branch settling under
pressure, something with mass redistributing at the edge of hearing. It comes from
behind her, not from beside or above. The exact character of the sound belongs to
the writing phase; what is settled is its quality — specific enough to warrant
turning, ordinary enough to remain genuinely unexplained.

She turns. There is nothing to see.

She keeps walking.

The scene ends in the distinction between two dispositions that must be kept separate:
she does not resolve the sound into an explanation, and she declines to let it stop
her. These are different things. She cannot promise herself the sound was nothing —
only that it is not going to slow her down. She is in the kind of place where she
cannot make the first promise, and she knows it. She makes the second one instead.

The humor that accompanied the stumble and the private observations of Beat 2 is
absent here. The beat is not light enough for a quip; the question does not close
neatly enough for a private verdict. The silence where a quip would normally go is
itself the legible register shift. It is not dramatic silence. It is simply the absence
of the ironic recovery the scene has trained the audience to expect.

*Register:* The season's first unresolved thing is planted and traveling forward with
her. It is hers alone, before the Wolf has arrived to add to it. She is in motion;
the scene ends.

## Immediate Pressures

**The errand as given commitment.** The basket is the yes she has already spoken.
She said she would go; grandmother is expecting her; the bread is warm now and will
be cooler later. The clock is already running. She is not watching it yet, but it is
present in the warmth she can feel through her hands: this care travels on a
timescale that can be spent.

**The pleasure that is its own stake.** The private pleasure in the jar is not
comfort before the real drama — it *is* the drama. Care that lands precisely because
it came from knowledge, rather than from instruction or duty, is exactly what
the encounter will slowly compete with. The audience must feel it here, in full,
before anything is spent.

**The question running alongside the forward motion.** She is not only delivering
provisions. She is finding out whether the judgment required to do it belongs to her.
The question does not have language yet — it arrives as a pressure, a checking-ahead
of where her feet are. The walk and the private inquiry proceed together.

**The navigational real uncertainty.** The fork is not an obstacle inserted to make
the Wolf's later help seem useful. It is a genuine navigational problem on thin
evidence, committed to with what is available. When the Wolf offers navigational
advice in Scene 2, it will land into a real uncertainty, not a manufactured one.
That genuineness must be present in Red's body at the fork — the slight engagement,
the quieter register, the particular quality of a person working through an actual
problem.

**The pattern of private recovery.** By the end of Beat 3, a pattern has been
established: private difficulty met by private ironic recovery. The absence of that
recovery in Beat 5 is not emptiness — it is information. What does not close there
is the first thing in this scene she has not been able to make bearable with language.

**The time margin she is spending.** Not urgency, not lateness — just the awareness
that the morning is finite and she is in it. Each decision point uses some of what
she has. She is not frantic; she is present to the cost of time.

## Sensory Vocabulary

These images and physical registers are available for the scene's expression. They
do not prescribe action; they name the field the character inhabits.

- **Hands and warmth:** The bread's warmth through the wrapping. The jar's cooler
  weight. Switching hands, adjusting grip, the weight migrating from left to right
  and back. The basket must be felt wherever time is spent.

- **Feet and ground:** Packed earth. Patches that have gone soft. The specific
  lurch of a root met too late to avoid cleanly. The heel seam giving way to cold
  water. The reset of footing after recovery. The committed stride at the fork —
  the weight moving forward before the argument has settled.

- **Eyes and light:** Strip pattern in the birch. The quieter, more horizontal
  light in the older trees. The unreliable reading at the fork when cloud cover
  shifts. The turn of the head at Beat 5, finding nothing. The look that does not
  resolve into explanation.

- **Private attention and candor:** Noticing a thing before deciding how to name it.
  A self-correction that redirects mid-thought. The specific pleasure of the jar
  that is not for a listener. The ironic phrase that follows the stumble, belonging
  to no one but her.

- **The absent quip:** The space at the end where the recovery-language would
  normally arrive and doesn't. This is as sensory as anything else in the scene —
  the listener knows what it sounds like when she makes something bearable, and
  this is what the alternative sounds like.

- **Time and warmth together:** The bread cooling is not metaphor. It is the actual
  condition: care that was warm when she left, carried through time. Both readings
  — care and clock — live in the same grip.

## Source-Grounded Character Context

**Red's pursuit** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/objective.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/objective.md)):
Deliver the provisions to grandmother through her own judgment. Care makes the errand
real; independence makes it personally important. She wants to carry that care through
the woods without having to earn passage by pleasing someone else. This scene is
that wish in its first and most unencumbered form — the errand, her pleasure in it,
her private candor, with no encounter yet to spend them.

**Red's hidden wish** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/hidden_objective.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/hidden_objective.md)):
To be trusted before she has proved herself, and to feel that trust taking root inside
her. The basket check in Beat 1 is the first expression of this wish — care enacted
for its own sake with no audience to confirm it. She wants to arrive with her own
account intact. This scene is where that account begins.

**Self-conflict** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/conflict_with_self.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/conflict_with_self.md)):
She thinks of herself as someone who can manage the journey; that belief can sustain
courage or make uncertainty feel like a private failure. The fork activates this
conflict: the provisional commitment is the form available to someone at the edge
of their terrain knowledge. Whether she registers it as competence or as evidence
of something falling short is the open question Beat 4 leaves for the writing phase.

**Environmental conflict** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/conflict_with_environment.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/conflict_with_environment.md)):
The woodland path asks for attention to distance, footing, and provisions. Home
supplies familiar rules; out here she must judge what those rules cannot settle.
Each beat gives that conflict its specific form: the known landmark stepped around
from memory, the unknown wet patch where the seam fails, the fork without an
authoritative instruction.

**Voice and register** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/line_of_thought.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/line_of_thought.md)):
Short bursts, fragments, quick pivots, self-corrections. Private candor runs ahead
of spoken courtesy. Ironic understatement in service of making small indignities
bearable. The voice demonstrations establish *Love that for me* as the scene's
formal template for the mud-shoe recovery — the line belongs to the writing phase,
the form belongs to the canon. In this scene she speaks only to herself, which
means her humor is probably funnier than anything she would offer a listener.

**Images** (from [`bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/line_of_images.md`](../../../bible/characters/little_red_riding_hood/line_of_images.md)):
Red cloth as the nearness of home; the basket as care with weight her hands can
recognize; the path as both direction and the room to choose the next step; the
thought of arriving carries grandmother. These are the scene's primary image field.

**Arc context** (from [`bible/dramatic_arcs.md`](../../../bible/dramatic_arcs.md)):
Red's discernment arc begins here, before any external pressure from the Wolf. The
gap the season will widen — between performed competence and felt competence, between
the internal account and the external one — opens in these first minutes alone. The
Wolf will find a character already practiced at carrying unresolved things alongside
forward purpose, already alert to the difference between performing ease and occupying it.

## Open Character Questions for Phase 6

These questions belong to Red's own expression and remain open for the beats to give
concrete form. Phase 6 will establish the ordered beat sequence; the roundtable is their
lived resolution.

1. **The exact form of the mud-shoe recovery.** The brief phrase that makes the
   indignity bearable — funny because it is hers alone, following this specific
   physical fact, in this particular private register. The canon supplies the form;
   the line is the writing phase's. What must come first is the unvarnished sensation.

2. **What Red notices at Beat 2.** One observation in the early woods — what her
   voice does with a specific detail, a self-correction at the edge of a thought,
   an opinion that has nowhere to hide because there is no audience. Brief and
   particular, with opinions.

3. **The physical form of the committed stride.** What does her body do at the
   moment the decision moves ahead of the deliberation? The quality of motion that
   makes the committed uncertainty legible without staging the argument — the exact
   way the basket shifts, the weight moves, the stride lengthens into the left path
   before the question is settled.

4. **The specific sound at the close.** One concrete sound — specific enough to
   warrant turning, ordinary enough to remain genuinely ambiguous. Not described as
   menacing; not explained. The writing phase must commit to its character: a branch,
   a settling, a redistribution of mass. The audience should be able to imagine it
   without knowing what made it.

5. **Whether the absence of humor is staged.** The scene has established, by Beat 3,
   a pattern: private difficulty met with ironic recovery. The silence where a quip
   would normally go in Beat 5 may be the most precisely legible register shift
   available. The writing phase should hold this as an active possibility and give it
   its concrete form — the pause that does not close, the absence that is information.

6. **Grandmother as a face at Beat 1.** The private pleasure in the jar contains a
   specific person: grandmother in autumn, returning to this particular flavor,
   someone whose preferences Red knows well enough to have gotten the thing right.
   How much of that face is present in the moment Red's hands check the cloth?
   The jar must carry a felt presence of the recipient before any other knowledge
   can intrude upon that intimacy.

## What This Scene Delivers to Scene 2

Red arrives at *The First Meeting* carrying two specific things:

**A provisional navigational commitment to the left fork.** Her best available
decision on thin evidence — not a mistake she is unaware of, not settled confidence.
The Wolf's navigational advice in Scene 2 will land into a real uncertainty, not a
manufactured one. That genuineness is load-bearing: it makes the Wolf's first
apparent usefulness honest.

**An unresolved sound she turned toward, could not explain, and has declined to let
slow her.** She is in the kind of place where she cannot promise herself that every
sound is nothing. She has only the second promise available: that this one is not going
to stop her. The first unresolved thing the act plants is hers alone, before the Wolf
has contributed any.

The basket is still warm. She is still moving. The season's measure is in her hands.
