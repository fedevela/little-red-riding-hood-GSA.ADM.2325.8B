## Dramatic continuity

### Threads entering

- **Red’s discernment — red / #B33A3A:** Red has already learned that a route can be chosen without being safe. Here she turns that unease into a repeatable practice: mark, ask for a concrete account, inspect, and compare.
- **The Wolf’s enclosure — moss / #52634A:** The Wolf knows the woods better than Red, but its knowledge is partial and its reading of her remains fallible. It can make useful terrain information feel like a reason to stay near its voice. The pause gives it access to her method, not authority over her.
- **The unanswered threshold — amber / #B98532:** Red earns a reliable doubt about one claim, not a safe route. The time spent proving the doubt makes the next decision more expensive.

### Development decision: one continuous route test

Use a fallen limb or branch dragged across the ordinary continuation at a fork, with a narrow visible trace slipping around it. A dark wet streak and a shallow dip interrupt the trace just beyond a recognizable split root. Red places a small twig crosswise at the fork, then asks the Wolf to account for three things in order: where the obstructed road continues, where the trace turns, and where the ground changes. This makes one material object carry the whole sequence.

The Wolf first claims that the branch marks a dangerous washout and that the trace is the only sensible way forward. When Red asks why the branch itself is dry on its upper side while the claimed washout lies beyond the wet streak, the Wolf narrows or revises the claim: perhaps the danger is a hidden dip beside the trace, not a break in the road. Red tests the dip with her stick or shoe and finds firm ground for the first step. That proves the Wolf’s first certainty incomplete; it does not prove the trace safe. The Wolf has learned that repeated contact with ground persuades Red, and can offer to call each next change or remain near enough to “protect” her from it.

The dependable gain is narrow and visible: the Wolf’s claim that the branch itself proves an impassable washout is not supported by the branch, wetness, or tested first ground. The cost is also visible: Red spends daylight and attention counting, asking, testing, and watching the Wolf revise. She has not revealed the destination, but her return mark and method reveal what kind of evidence she trusts.

### Change the audience must register

At entry, the obstruction and basket divide Red’s attention between care and progress. During the test, her mark and repeated checks give her a method she can own. At the pivotal turn, the audience must hold two simultaneous facts: Red can now doubt one specific explanation for good reason, and the Wolf has used the pause to learn how to make future guidance more intimate and costly. The route remains physically open but not certified safe.
