# Yesod Dramatic-Action Brief — The Attention Cost

## Scene frame

`S1E1-Scene-06-Attention-Cost` is one sustained, playable encounter in Act 1,
immediately beyond the split alder and ending at the next visible threshold.
The late-day, post-rain trace narrows between wet bracken, a root-raised bank,
and dark standing water. A visible opening or fallen limb lies ahead. The
immediate footing can be inspected and crossed; no part of the route is
declared safe. Red retains the basket and her destination. The Wolf remains at
a readable distance and does not touch, block, redirect, compel, or take the
basket.

The scene’s dramatic test is simultaneous gain and cost. Red can distinguish a
real physical difficulty from the Wolf’s claim that its preferred help is
necessary. She can refuse closeness and keep moving. The Wolf’s response then
makes its welcome less stable and adds an urgent claim that has an observable
sign but no complete verification from Red’s position. Each check protects her
judgment while spending time and attention owed to the errand. The threshold
must arrive with the basket, route, and right to refuse still hers, while the
Wolf’s danger and next movement remain unresolved.

## Authorship and observation boundaries

The fields below establish stimuli, available knowledge, pressure, and
affordances, not exact thoughts, dialogue, gestures, or decisions. Red and the
Wolf author their own responses from what each can perceive. The Wolf may infer
from Red’s visible tests, pauses, answers, and movement, but it cannot know her
private motive. Red may observe the Wolf’s position, voice, posture, and
changing treatment, but its predatory intention remains a claim to be judged,
not established knowledge. Mother and grandmother remain offstage presences
carried by Red’s purpose; they have no performed action in this scene.

offer; Red tests and refuses; the Wolf’s warmth changes; an uncertain sign is
enlarged into danger; Red advances a few steps, looks back, compares, and
advances again. The Wolf can use stillness, a low reminder, or apparent concern
to keep the warning active, but it must leave the route physically open. The
last image is forward ownership under a divided gaze, not proof that Red has
