## *The Claim Against the Path*

## Beat: The Thinned Wood at Morning
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B001] -->

SCENE_HEADING: EXT. PATH — PAST THE OLD GROWTH — CONTINUING MORNING

LIGHTING: The canopy has yielded. Autumn sun arrives at a flatter angle than the long horizontal planes of the old-growth section — direct patches falling on the path surface, longer shadows from the remaining trees, the sky accessible in open stretches between what crown remains. Not dramatic. Morning continued.

AUDIO: The acoustic is more open than the old-growth shelter. Individual birds registrable — direction audible, not diffuse. An occasional creak from the old-wood canopy behind carries into this space with air around it. The Wolf's voice, when it comes, sits in ordinary open air.

ACTION: Two figures on a path. The earth underfoot has the yielding quality the Wolf described at the close of Episode 1 — not treacherous, not muddy, just softer than the packed old-growth surface, the ground receiving each step with a slight give. Red carries the basket in her left hand. The bread inside is cool — not cold, but the warmth from the gate is genuinely gone. They have been walking together, or in close proximity, for some time. The company has not broken.

ACTION: The path's character here: the second bend is behind them. The old growth has fully thinned. The path runs left at this section, as the Wolf described it would, after the canopy's final opening. The ford is also behind her — well behind, before the second bend. Red has been reading this new terrain as she has read every terrain this morning: forward, before it's underfoot.

ACTION: An opportunity for the Wolf to offer a remark in the register of continued practical company — the unhurried Southern cadence, something drawn from the ordinary facts of walking in this section of path, warm in the idiom of a creature at ease in this terrain. The remark carries the tone of continued walking-together conversation, not a subject change. Red receives it in motion.

DIALOGUE: [WOLF — ordinary walking exchange, practical and unhurried, in the register of the morning's prior company]

ACTION: Red's reply, if she gives one, is in the same register — a brief return, the kind of conversational surface that keeps company without giving ground. Her attention is forward on the path.

DIALOGUE: [RED — brief, forward, conversational surface maintained]

## Beat: The Ford Ahead
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B002] -->

ACTION: The Wolf introduces the ford. Its register is practical concern — not alarmist, not sudden, not a subject change that requires marking. The language of a creature passing on local knowledge as a courtesy. The delivery settles into the path-talk that has preceded it.

AUDIO: The Wolf's cadence: unhurried, Southern. Clauses unfold comfortably. The important detail — the position of the ford, the condition of the near bank — is organized so the location arrives before the hazard's particulars. The word *ford* or *crossing* is placed early in the claim, before the bank's condition is described.

DIALOGUE: [WOLF — names the ford's position as past the second bend; describes the near bank as taking overnight runoff; specifies the left side where the bank flattens gives in the top inch when weighted; recommends keeping right to the stones; names the position again as where the path comes back left after the old growth has fully thinned]

ACTION: The specificity is the claim's authority. A vague warning could be placed anywhere. This one has a location and a mechanism and a recommended alternative. The Wolf delivers this in the manner of its verified directions through the birchwood and past the root-crossing cluster — the same idiom, the same confidence.

PARENTHETICAL: (The Wolf's posture throughout the claim: no step toward her, no gesture away from the path ahead. The manner of a creature imparting known information about a route it has walked. Unhurried.)

## Beat: The Word That Will Not Land
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B003] -->

ACTION: Red is walking. The ford — *ford*, or *crossing*, whichever word the Wolf used — arrives in her hearing and attempts to locate itself on the mental record she has been building of this morning's walk. It does not land. The word is understood in its general meaning. It has no referent in front of her.

ACTION: Her mental map of the walk has its ford, and it is behind her. Before the second bend. The near bank was firm underfoot — ordinary dry bank, no give, the approach entirely normal. Her hand went briefly to the basket handle before settling back. The crossing took no special management. That crossing was a fact she handled and continued past.

CAMERA: The gaze-drop. Her eyes move to the ground ahead of her feet — not toward the Wolf, not a social check. The direction her gaze goes when the terrain's accounting has not balanced. This gesture is established in the performed text: she looks at the path surface when something in the terrain's reckoning has not resolved. Here there is no root ahead. No obstacle requiring the look. The mismatch is not in the path. It is in the description.

ACTION: Three steps of continued walking. The basket shifts slightly in her left hand — not a check, just the ordinary redistribution of a weight carried in motion. The bread is cool. These three steps are the body working before the mind has assembled what the body found.

AUDIO: No vocal response from Red during these three steps. The Wolf's voice, if it continues, arrives in the space of her continued walking.

PARENTHETICAL: (The floating is not a thought she has. It is a failure of fit: the description is attempting to settle on her mental path and cannot find where to settle. The shape of the wrongness has a location before she has a name for it. The scene does not name the wrongness for her here. It expresses it through the gaze-drop and the three steps.)

## Beat: The Probe
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B004] -->

ACTION: The probe forms and arrives. It is a navigation question — the kind she has been using all episode, the lateral re-ask that sounds like checking her own map rather than interrogating the Wolf's claim.

ACTION: The probe contains the word *crossing* or *ford* — the word that floated. It approaches the inconsistency from the angle of personal error: she might have the sequence wrong, she might be misremembering the order of bends. It names the landmark the Wolf gave (*the second bend* or equivalent) inside a question whose shape says she is asking help to confirm something for herself.

PARENTHETICAL: (The uncertainty voice demonstration texture applies: a fragment with a self-correction mid-sentence. She is not fully assembled. The uncertainty in the probe is partly performed, partly real — she is confirming the inconsistency by asking, not announcing it as already confirmed. The probe sounds like her checking herself. It functions as a test. The Wolf will read apparent uncertainty as an opening. Red knows what she is doing with the question.)

DIALOGUE: [RED — navigation question; contains the word for the crossing; names the second bend or its equivalent as the landmark; the form of someone confirming her own map rather than challenging the claim; fragment with a self-correction mid-sentence]

PARENTHETICAL: (The probe does not arrive before the three steps. The body found the wrongness; the question forms from what the body found. The cadence here is shorter than her ordinary conversational ease — she is doing more active work than any preceding stretch of path.)

## Beat: The Quick Answer
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B005] -->

ACTION: The Wolf's answer comes quickly. The quickness precedes any elaboration — the elaboration follows the quick answer rather than accompanying it. This is the tell.

PARENTHETICAL: (A person who genuinely knows a path gives the landmark, an approximate distance, maybe a seasonal detail. What follows here is a reframing: the ford's position is adjusted — its relationship to the turnings shifts — in a way that makes the original description vanish rather than stand corrected. The original claim does not get a correction; it gets a replacement. The landmark moves.)

AUDIO: A slight compression in the Wolf's cadence at the moment of the quick answer — briefer than the comfortable unfolding of the original claim. Then, as the revised description settles, the warmth reconstitutes. The warmth after the compression sounds like the warmth before it. This is what makes the revision convincing to a less attentive listener. The compression is the tell for an attending audience.

DIALOGUE: [WOLF — quick answer; adjusts the ford's position relative to the turnings; the new description places the ford at a different feature or at a modified relationship to the second bend, in a way that makes the original claim's position vanish rather than stand corrected; the new description has its own small internal wrongness; the warmth reconstitutes in the elaboration that follows]

PARENTHETICAL: (The Wolf has spent a certainty of manner. The specific quality of authority that belonged to the original description — confident, specific, the language of a creature that has walked this path — required that description to go unchallenged. The quick recovery is a resource expenditure. The Wolf does not know this. It reads its own quickness as competence.)

## Beat: Both Claims in Inventory
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B006] -->

ACTION: Red receives the revised description. She does not name either claim aloud — neither the original position nor its displacement. She holds both. The holding is active, requiring the same quiet effort as every other piece of private inventory she has accumulated since the birchwood fork.

CAMERA: Her face during the Wolf's revision: attending, forward, not visibly different from the face she brought to every previous practical exchange. No changed grip. No held breath. No pause that runs long enough to carry weight in it. The exterior gives nothing away.

ACTION: The inconsistency sharpens during these few seconds. She has the original claim: the ford past the second bend. She has the revised claim: the ford at a different position, the landmark moved rather than corrected. The second description has its own small wrongness. She has both of them now, held alongside each other.

PARENTHETICAL: (The preparation's retrieval shape is present, in the form she will be able to carry out of this scene: the ford before the second bend; the Wolf's description placing it after; the near bank firm at the actual crossing. Three facts, not a feeling. This private inventory does not announce itself through any exterior signal.)

## Beat: The Thank-You
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B007] -->

ACTION: Red thanks the Wolf for the information. The thank-you arrives in its ordinary shape and in its ordinary timing — the same register as every previous thank-you in the encounter. Not warmer than those. Not colder. Genuinely polite. The texture established in Episode 1, Scene 3: *that helps, thank you.* Nothing in its surface announces the filing beneath it.

DIALOGUE: [RED — thank-you in the ordinary register; nothing in its form, timing, or tone announces what has just been filed; spoken courtesy covering what her private inventory now holds]

PARENTHETICAL: (The Wolf reads the thank-you and the resumed walking as evidence that the revision landed. It does not know what she filed. The irony is available to the audience and invisible to the Wolf. The thank-you must arrive without a pause before it that runs long enough to carry weight. The Wolf's misreading of this courtesy is the scene's structural engine.)

## Beat: The Resumed Walk
<!-- RESOLVES [BEAT issue-71-B008] -->

ACTION: Red resumes walking. Forward. Basket in her left hand. The bread is cool. Grandmother is still at the path's other end.

ACTION: No backward glance. No tightened grip. No visible marker of what she is carrying. The forward step — not the lightened pace of a person whose question found its answer, not a heavied pace of a person who has confirmed a danger. The continued steady pace of a person carrying what she came in with plus something new. The distinction is fine enough that the Wolf cannot see it. An attending audience can.

CAMERA: The path ahead. She is reading it the way she has read every surface this morning: forward, before it's underfoot. The path is ordinary. The morning continues. The exchange has closed on its surface as a question that found its answer.

AUDIO: The ambient of the thinned canopy section: individual birds, the occasional distant creak from the old wood behind, the open air. Nothing changed by what happened inside it.

ACTION: The Wolf continues in its manner, unaware of what has entered inventory. Its assumption — that its expertise claim remains intact, that the courtesy it received confirms the revision landed — is wrong. The act continues.

PARENTHETICAL: (The audience carries both the original claim and its revision, in the same order Red filed them. They have watched the three steps, the gaze-drop, the probe, the too-quick answer, the displaced landmark, the thank-you, the resumed walking. They know what she knows. The scene ends with the Wolf unaware of what has just been transferred into Red's private keeping.)
