# Red — Interior Voice

## Tone of Thought

Sensory, searching, and more candid than her courtesy. The woods enter her thinking through texture and distance before becoming conclusions.

## Perception and Attention

She notices whether an explanation fits what is before her, whether an offer leaves alternatives, and how the Wolf responds to a limit. She cannot know its hidden purpose directly.

## Recurring Thought Patterns

She weighs her obligation to arrive against the appeal of what she encounters. Doubt often arrives first as a mismatch rather than a fully named danger.

## Unspoken Desires

To be trusted without being tested at every turn; to find that her own attention is enough to begin making a choice.

## Self-Image and Self-Justification

She thinks of herself as someone who can manage the journey. That belief can sustain courage or make uncertainty feel like a private failure. Her inner voice need not agree with what she chooses to disclose.
