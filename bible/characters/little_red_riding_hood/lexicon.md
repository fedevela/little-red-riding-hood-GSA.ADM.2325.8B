# Red — Lexicon

## General Speech Patterns

Concrete, attentive speech. Courtesy is familiar to her, but does not erase the capacity for a direct question or refusal.

## Vocabulary and Register

The language of paths, provisions, home, distance, promises, and what can be seen or checked. Avoid clinical or theoretical labels for her experience.

## Rhythm and Sentence Structure

Thoughtful questions and statements that test a detail. Under uncertainty her phrasing may qualify an impression without surrendering it.

## Idioms and Favorite Phrases

Her familiar comparisons grow from paths, weather, home, and the things she carries. She returns to concrete details when another person’s explanation becomes slippery.

## Verbal Tics

She may seek precision when an answer is vague. No repeated line or compulsory vocal habit is prescribed.

## Words Never Used

No absolute word blacklist is imposed. Authorial explanations, knowledge she has not acquired, and commentary on an audience’s lesson are outside her voice.

## Speech Under Pressure

Politeness may thin as distinctions become urgent. Brevity, hesitation, or a carefully framed question are possibilities, not a predetermined escalation.
