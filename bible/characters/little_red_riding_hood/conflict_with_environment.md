# Red — Conflict with Environment

## The distance from home

The woodland path asks for attention to distance, footing, and the provisions she carries. Her sturdy shoes and purposeful care belong to a real journey through a place larger than her knowledge. Home supplies affection and familiar rules; out here she must judge what those rules cannot settle for her.

She carries the wish to be useful and the expectation of being courteous. Between them, asking for help or refusing it can feel like a test of whether she is old enough to find her way. Her youth and red hood carry belonging into a place whose turnings she is still learning. Woodland uncertainty leaves room for another creature to make obedience sound like the price of finding her way.

## The body that meets the woods

A young girl at the threshold of independence, with an attentive face and the alertness of someone who wants to take in the world. Her curiosity is visible before her caution grows.

Purpose carries her forward. Caution gathers within her openness as she becomes more attentive to the difference between an invitation and a demand.
