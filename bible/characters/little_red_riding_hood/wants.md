# Red — Wants

## Conscious Want

To deliver the provisions to grandmother and complete the journey through her own judgment.

## Underlying Motivation

Care gives the errand meaning; independence makes it personally important.

## Deep Need

To grant her own perception weight without first securing another person’s approval.

## Obstacles and Competing Desires

The Wolf’s offers can meet real wishes for companionship, reassurance, or an easier journey. Accepting something useful and retaining freedom may become difficult to separate.

## Personal Stakes

Her safety, her ability to choose, and her confidence that she can respond when a familiar rule no longer answers the situation. No completed growth or escape is promised.
