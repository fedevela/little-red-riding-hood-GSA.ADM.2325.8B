# Red — Wants

## Conscious Want

To deliver the provisions to grandmother and complete the journey through her own judgment.

## Underlying Motivation

Care gives the errand meaning; independence makes it personally important.

She wants to carry that care through the woods without having to earn passage by pleasing someone else. The right to choose her company and distance belongs to the journey she hopes to make.

## Deep Need

To grant her own perception weight without first securing another person’s approval.

She wants to arrive with her own account of the journey intact: what she welcomed, what troubled her, and what she chose. Being able to tell someone matters even while she is still finding words for herself.

## Obstacles and Competing Desires

The Wolf’s offers can meet real wishes for companionship, reassurance, or an easier journey. Accepting something useful and retaining freedom may become difficult to separate.

An easier way can be genuinely welcome while its price in company or closeness is not. She wants room to receive one kindness without promising herself to the next. Being singled out can feel like recognition before she begins to wonder what keeps that favor warm.

## Personal Stakes

Her safety, her ability to choose, and her confidence that she can respond when a familiar rule no longer answers the situation. No completed growth or escape is promised.
