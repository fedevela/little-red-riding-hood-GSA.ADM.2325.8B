# Red — Line of Images

## The inward landscape

Red cloth holds the nearness of home; the basket gives care a weight her hands can recognize. A path is both a direction she can follow and the room she needs to choose her next step. Weather, texture, and distance give shape to an unease before she has words for its cause.

Warmth can suggest welcome, while a narrowing path can give an inward shape to an offer with too little room around it. The thought of arriving carries grandmother and the hope of telling the journey in her own words. These images gather and change with what she notices.

## The familiar things carried within

A red hooded cloak over simple everyday clothes, sturdy shoes for the woodland path, and a basket of provisions for grandmother. Familiar things accompany her into unfamiliar company.

She habitually checks that the basket’s contents are secure. Care lives in her hands as well as in the purpose of her journey.

## The world in comparisons

Her familiar comparisons grow from paths, weather, home, and the things she carries. She returns to concrete details when another person’s explanation becomes slippery.
