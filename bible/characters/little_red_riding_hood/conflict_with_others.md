# Red — Conflict with Others

## Where their wishes collide

The Wolf’s offers can meet real wishes for companionship, reassurance, or an easier journey. Accepting something useful and retaining freedom may become difficult to separate.

An easier way can be genuinely welcome while its price in company or closeness is not. She wants room to receive one kindness without promising herself to the next. Being singled out can feel like recognition before she begins to wonder what keeps that favor warm.

## The changing encounter

She compares what is said with what she notices. Questions are one way she preserves room for herself; agreement may be provisional rather than wholehearted.

Her attention becomes more selective. She may delay an answer, withhold information, or name a contradiction.

An explanation that changes under questioning unsettles her. Concern that dismisses her judgment, or an offer that makes refusal sound like wrongdoing, presses against her wish to trust.

She can try to settle uncertainty privately or make a refusal gentler than she feels.

## What can be revealed

Red weighs how much of her wishes and doubts to reveal. A question, a pause, or a guarded answer may show more than she intended.

Revealing uncertainty can invite a more tempting offer; naming a doubt can also give her boundary a voice.
