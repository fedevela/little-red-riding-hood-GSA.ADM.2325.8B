# Red — Objective

## The pursuit

To deliver the provisions to grandmother and complete the journey through her own judgment.

Care gives the errand meaning; independence makes it personally important.

She wants to carry that care through the woods without having to earn passage by pleasing someone else. The right to choose her company and distance belongs to the journey she hopes to make.

## What is at stake

Her safety, her ability to choose, and her confidence that she can respond when a familiar rule no longer answers the situation.
