# Wolf — Conflict with Others

## Where their wishes collide

Red can question, refuse, reveal less than expected, or recognize contradictions. The Wolf’s wish to preserve a harmless appearance limits how plainly it can assert control.

## The changing encounter

It tests what kind of offer matters to Red and adjusts when the fit is poor. Interest, assistance, flattery, and urgency offer different ways toward what it wants.

It is inclined to let warmth follow receptiveness, making favor seem to belong to the Red who welcomes it. A limit can draw a cooler answer or a more costly offer. Red’s response can unsettle its reading of the relationship.

A refusal may provoke greater charm, a changed rationale, or less well-concealed impatience. Resistance can frustrate its designs.

Questions it struggles to answer consistently, information Red withholds, and limits that survive its apparent kindness leave its influence uncertain.

It can recast an offer, minimize a contradiction, or appeal to a different concern. It can make urgency out of a claim, hoping its words will hurry her judgment.

## What can be revealed

The Wolf keeps its intention beneath the claims it offers. An inconsistency or a glimpse of impatience may give Red reason to look more closely.

Exposure threatens the kindly appearance on which its influence depends. It must contend with what Red has come to suspect.
