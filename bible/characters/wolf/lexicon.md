# Wolf — Lexicon

## General Speech Patterns

Attentive and apparently accommodating. It makes room for Red’s words in order to learn how an offer might reach her.

## Vocabulary and Register

Help, ease, safety, familiarity, concern, and capability furnish its public vocabulary. The language of hunger belongs to its concealed purpose and need not be spoken.

## Rhythm and Sentence Structure

Measured explanations and questions that appear to invite choice. Its phrasing can quietly narrow the available terms without declaring that narrowing.

## Idioms and Favorite Phrases

It borrows the vocabulary of an ordinary woodland journey, making its advice sound familiar and practical. The familiarity responds to what Red has actually revealed.

## Verbal Tics

It tends to return attention to the usefulness of its proposal when its motives are questioned. This is a tendency, not a required verbal maneuver.

## Words Never Used

No absolute word blacklist is imposed. It does not casually confess its concealed design, narrate the moral, or speak with knowledge it has not acquired.

## Speech Under Pressure

Its apparent openness can become more conditional; explanation can carry an edge of entitlement. The degree and timing of exposure remain performance choices.

Original characterization for this adaptation, developed under the partner-approved two-character premise. These details are adaptation choices, not claims about a particular published telling. See [project provenance](../../project.md#authoritative-sources).
