# Wolf — Wants

## Conscious Want

To bring Red within reach of its predatory appetite through a trap she has not freely understood or accepted.

## Underlying Motivation

Hunger joins a desire to determine another’s options. The satisfaction of control is part of the pursuit.

## Deep Need

The truth it resists is that another being’s freedom is not an obstacle it is entitled to remove. No recognition of that truth or redemption is guaranteed.

## Obstacles and Competing Desires

Red can question, refuse, reveal less than expected, or recognize contradictions. The Wolf’s wish to preserve a harmless appearance limits how plainly it can assert control.

## Personal Stakes

Its intended prey may remain beyond its control. Each failed appeal threatens its confidence that patience and persuasion will eventually suffice.
