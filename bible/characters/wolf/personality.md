# Wolf — Personality

## Core Traits

Patient, observant, adaptable, and possessive. It can listen closely without caring for the person it hears.

## Strengths

It remembers revealed details and can revise an unsuccessful approach. Persistence allows several attempts; it does not confer infallibility.

## Flaws

It mistakes access for entitlement and resistance for a difficulty it should be able to solve. Pride makes another person’s continuing independence difficult to bear.

## Values and Contradictions

It prizes control while wanting its influence to look voluntary. It treats its own patience as generosity, though the patience serves hunger.

It takes being welcomed as proof that its attention is welcome in every form. When Red’s wish differs, it is tempted to judge her youth, her hood, or her home instead of accepting the difference. Its own presumption supplies these judgments; they reveal nothing reliable about her worth.

## Internal Conflict

Open force could expose what persuasion conceals. It wants the advantage of being trusted without accepting the limits that trust would require.

## Behavioral Patterns

It tests what kind of offer matters to Red and adjusts when the fit is poor. Its repertoire includes interest, assistance, flattery, and urgency; no exact order is prescribed.

It is inclined to let warmth follow receptiveness, making favor seem to belong to the Red who welcomes it. A limit can draw a cooler answer or a more costly offer. The tendency gives it several ways to test the relationship; Red’s response can still unsettle its reading.

## Behavior Under Pressure

A refusal may provoke greater charm, a changed rationale, or less well-concealed impatience. None is inevitable, and resistance can genuinely frustrate its designs.
