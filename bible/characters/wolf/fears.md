# Wolf — Fears

## Greatest Fear

Losing influence before it has secured the conditions of the trap.

## Secondary Fears

Being recognized clearly; finding that Red’s refusal persists; discovering that its reading of her was wrong.

## Triggers

Questions it cannot answer consistently, information Red withholds, and limits that remain in place despite its apparent kindness.

## Avoidance Tactics

It can recast an offer, minimize a contradiction, or appeal to a different concern. Manufactured urgency is one available strategy, not an established external emergency.

## Cost of Avoidance

Revisions can expose the underlying design. The more it insists on its helpfulness, the harder it may become to preserve that appearance.
