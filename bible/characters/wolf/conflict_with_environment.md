# Wolf — Conflict with Environment

## The reach of familiar ground

The Wolf knows the woods and would make that familiarity a claim upon Red’s passage. Distance and the onward purpose of her errand leave its influence uncertain: she has somewhere to go that belongs to her own care and purpose.

To turn woodland uncertainty into dependence, it must make its own terms resemble shelter. A danger named with enough confidence might draw Red toward the safety it offers. The urgency serves its appetite.

## The body that meets the woods

A lean Wolf with dark, coarse fur, a long muzzle, and watchful eyes. Its bodily strength gives weight to the patience it presents as gentleness.

It spends little effort without purpose and is comfortable with stillness. Its patience can feel reassuring until that same attention begins to feel possessive.
