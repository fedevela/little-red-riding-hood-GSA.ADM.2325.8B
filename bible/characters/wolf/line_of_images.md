# Wolf — Line of Images

## The inward landscape

Its images gather around nearness, reach, and the distance still left between appetite and its object. Attention becomes ground gained; an unanswered question leaves a gap it would like to close. It imagines an offer as a path toward itself and safety as a shelter whose terms it could keep in its own paws.

The listening tilt and quiet coat belong to the image of gentleness it hopes to present. Beneath that image lies hunger, willing to borrow the look of care. It pictures the welcome it hopes to obtain, filling the distance between them with its own desire.

## The familiar things carried within

The Wolf wears its own coat of fur. Its manner supplies the disguise: concern softens the appearance of an appetite that remains its own.

Its attention can feel unusually complete. Appetite exists beneath that attention even when Red cannot yet identify it.

It holds its head at a listening tilt, attending closely to whatever seems useful. The habit carries both apparent interest and the concentration of pursuit.

## The world in comparisons

It borrows the vocabulary of an ordinary woodland journey, making its advice sound familiar and practical. The familiarity responds to what Red has actually revealed.
