# Wolf — Line of Thought

## How perception becomes a decision

Appraising, patient, and self-excusing. It attends to the space between what Red wants and what she is willing to accept.

It notices disclosed obligations, reactions to offers, and signs of unease. It reads those signs for an opening, sometimes mistaking a hesitation for the answer it wants.

It measures whether an approach increases influence, then searches for a different appeal when it fails. Refusal is information to it, though not always information it welcomes.

It counts the attention it wins as ground gained. It can call a pause in Red’s errand companionship and the effort she spends watching it interest. These names spare it the trouble of asking whether she wanted to give that part of her journey away.

It imagines itself as patient and practical. It tells itself that a choice made under its pressure still belongs entirely to Red. That explanation lets it enjoy influence while assigning the weight of the choice to her.

It imagines favorable treatment as something Red earns by being receptive. If she withdraws, it can make its own coldness seem like her doing. Beneath that explanation is its wish to decide both the price of kindness and who deserves to receive it.

## How thought reaches speech

Attentive and apparently accommodating. It makes room for Red’s words in order to learn how an offer might reach her.

Help, ease, safety, familiarity, concern, and capability furnish its public vocabulary. Hunger stays beneath the words it offers as reassurance.

Measured explanations and questions that appear to invite choice. Its phrasing can quietly narrow the available terms without declaring that narrowing.

It tends to return attention to the usefulness of its proposal when its motives are questioned.

Its apparent openness can become more conditional; explanation can carry an edge of entitlement. Impatience can roughen the gentleness it has worked to sustain.
