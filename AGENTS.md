# Creative Project Instructions

## Canon and Creative Scope

Read `bible/README.md` and its linked foundation documents before developing the
story. The human partner determines canon. Develop dramatic particulars within
that foundation; bring contradictions or changes to foundational choices back
to the partner. Familiarity with another telling does not establish canon here.

`bible/characters.md` introduces the two performing characters. Their stable IDs
are `little_red_riding_hood` and `wolf`; each has seven profile files beneath
`bible/characters/`, organized by the director’s dimensions in
[bible/README.md](bible/README.md#the-directors-seven-character-dimensions).
Use those dimensions as the complete character structure. Mother and grandmother may be mentioned offstage. The
performing cast remains Red and the Wolf.

The current assignment is Season 1 of the theatrical work. Develop its scenes,
dialogue, tactics, and physical realization within the bible's constraints.
Several attempts precede the Wolf's final trap, and Red's responses materially
affect its approach.

Preserve the unresolved ending: capture, escape, rescue, and reconciliation remain
unconfirmed, including in any epilogue. The threshold need not be a literal door
movement. Keep the mediator and discussion procedure outside the written work;
the audience's answer remains its own.

## Conceptual Reception

Read [GSA Concepts — Dramaturgy First](bible/project.md#gsa-concepts--dramaturgy-first) when planning
or reviewing Season 1. It preserves the partner's intended coverage of the
supplied source ideas, including the distinctions between conditional help,
changed treatment after a response, the burden on Red's purpose, and favor tied
to closeness. Use its mapping as authorial memory, prioritizing dramaturgy over rigid source wording.

Translate those ideas into the characters' relationship and the language of their
world. Keep source labels and institutional terminology in reference notes.
Review the conceptual coverage honestly, including the limited analogy for 1.4,
while preserving two performing characters and the unresolved ending. The mapping is authorial memory; roundtable access and observation boundaries
apply when translating it into character context. During review, check each of
its six source rows for a felt tension in the encounter. Develop gaps through
the characters, allowing several ideas to inhabit the same exchange. Coverage
does not require one incident per provision or the source’s vocabulary.

Keep bureaucratic definitions, institutional language, and compliance phrasing
out of the handoff and story. The source’s sexual dimensions are received as
unwanted personal closeness and conditional favor; no sexual events or mutual
intimate relationship are established. The 1.4 analogy remains partial, within
the two-character cast.

## Developing the Foundation

Each episode’s `script.md` contains its complete manuscript: front matter, acts,
scene headings, and all beats within those scenes. Keep scenes and beats within
that episode document. Only `script.md` files belong beneath `Script/`.
Keep every supporting writing artifact under the sibling `Script artifacts/` tree,
preserving season and episode structure. Quote the folder name in shell commands.
The blank manuscript scaffold remains beneath `Script/season_template/`; its
outline guides live beneath `Script artifacts/season_template/`.

Use the character profiles as sustained desires and responsive tendencies. Scene
objectives, tactics, dialogue, and actions develop through the encounter; profile
examples establish neither a fixed sequence nor required gestures or lines.
Several attempts must fail or succeed only partly before the final trap. Keep
Red’s resistance consequential and the Wolf’s reading fallible.

Preserve the bible’s physical and social foundations. A speaking Wolf is part of
this tale; mind-reading, spells, transformations, magical compulsion, and devices
that settle the conflict are not established. The Wolf’s invented dangers remain
claims until supported by the world. Character images supply associations, not
new biographical events. Bring changes to these foundations to the partner.

Keep character speech grounded in each speaker’s perceptions and voice. Private
thoughts can differ from spoken words; the Wolf’s self-justifications express its
own reasoning. Avoid authorial lessons spoken through characters. Mother and
grandmother remain offstage, without profiles, performed dialogue, or independent
arcs. Preserve Red’s dignity and the Wolf’s responsibility for its choices.

Bible revision develops foundations; scenes, beats, and scripts belong under
`Script/` when story writing is assigned. Arc colors identify planning threads.

## Workflow and Deliverables

Use the creative-writer workflow's applicable stages and artifact contracts,
including its human decision points. Before an automated run, consult the
[runtime compatibility note](README.md#runtime-compatibility): the character-file
contract and scene-level delivery still need to be adapted to these dimensions
and the episode manuscript. Develop Season 1 materials beneath
`Script/Season_01/`, keeping the bible as the canonical foundation. Trace scene
responsibilities to assigned story beats. Prepare scene handoffs when their real
inputs are available; writing instructions alone do not establish readiness for
performance.

Phases 7 and 8 prepare dramatic action and preserve scene materials. Phase 9 is
the director's roundtable; phase 10 is separate revision. Scene materials and
runtime discovery are described in the project README.

Assess the resulting work for two distinct character voices, the Wolf's gradual
and adaptable pursuit, consequential agency for Red, continuity with the bible,
and a credible danger whose decisive outcome remains open.

## Character Knowledge and Roundtable Access

Distinguish private thoughts from what another character can observe, learn, or
reasonably infer. During roundtable performance, each character may access only
its own folder within `bible/characters/` and use only its supplied context and
eligible observations. Do not read, list, or search sibling character folders,
including through repository-wide searches or alternate paths.

The director receives the full briefing. Actor turns remain response-only; this
folder instruction does not grant tool use or file writes and is not filesystem
isolation.
