# Creative Project Instructions

## Canon and Creative Scope

Read `bible/README.md` and its linked foundation documents before developing the
story. The human partner determines canon. Develop dramatic particulars within
that foundation; bring contradictions or changes to foundational choices back
to the partner. Familiarity with another telling does not establish canon here.

`bible/characters.md` introduces the two performing characters. Their stable IDs
are `little_red_riding_hood` and `wolf`; each has seven profile files beneath
`bible/characters/`. Mother and grandmother may be mentioned offstage. The
performing cast remains Red and the Wolf.

The current assignment is Season 1 of the theatrical work. Develop its scenes,
dialogue, tactics, and physical realization within the bible's constraints.
Several attempts precede the Wolf's final trap, and Red's responses materially
affect its approach.

Preserve the unresolved ending: capture, escape, rescue, and reconciliation remain
unconfirmed, including in any epilogue. The threshold need not be a literal door
movement. Keep the mediator and discussion procedure outside the written work;
the audience's answer remains its own.

## Conceptual Reception

Read [GSA Concepts — Dramaturgy First](bible/project.md#gsa-concepts--dramaturgy-first) when planning
or reviewing Season 1. It preserves the partner's intended coverage of the
supplied source ideas, including the distinctions between conditional help,
changed treatment after a response, the burden on Red's purpose, and favor tied
to closeness. Use its mapping as authorial memory, prioritizing dramaturgy over rigid source wording.

Translate those ideas into the characters' relationship and the language of their
world. Keep source labels and institutional terminology in reference notes.
Review the conceptual coverage honestly, including the limited analogy for 1.4,
while preserving two performing characters and the unresolved ending. The section
supplies no scene sequence or additional character knowledge; roundtable access
and observation boundaries still apply.

## Workflow and Deliverables

Use the creative-writer workflow's applicable stages and artifact contracts,
including its human decision points. Develop Season 1 materials beneath
`Script/Season_01/`, keeping the bible as the canonical foundation. Trace scene
responsibilities to assigned story beats. Prepare scene handoffs when their real
inputs are available; writing instructions alone do not establish readiness for
performance.

Phases 7 and 8 prepare dramatic action and preserve scene materials. Phase 9 is
the director's roundtable; phase 10 is separate revision. Scene materials and
runtime discovery are described in the project README.

Assess the resulting work for two distinct character voices, the Wolf's gradual
and adaptable pursuit, consequential agency for Red, continuity with the bible,
and a credible danger whose decisive outcome remains open.

## Character Knowledge and Roundtable Access

Distinguish private thoughts from what another character can observe, learn, or
reasonably infer. During roundtable performance, each character may access only
its own folder within `bible/characters/` and use only its supplied context and
eligible observations. Do not read, list, or search sibling character folders,
including through repository-wide searches or alternate paths.

The director receives the full briefing. Actor turns remain response-only; this
folder instruction does not grant tool use or file writes and is not filesystem
isolation.
