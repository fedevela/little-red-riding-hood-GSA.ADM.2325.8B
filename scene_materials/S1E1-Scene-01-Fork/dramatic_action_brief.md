## Scene frame

The scene begins with Red already carrying a purposeful errand. Grandmother is
offstage; the provisions, the finite daylight, and Red’s care make that
relationship theatrically present without giving it a speaking part. The
ordinary road is blocked by a fallen tree. The alternative trace is visibly
wet and broken but testable only at its entrance. The Wolf’s terrain knowledge
can be accurate without becoming authority.

The brief preserves open authorship. It supplies what can reach each character,
what each can reasonably know, and what the space permits; it does not select
Red’s wording, the Wolf’s tactics, or either character’s private response.
The return mark is a modest record, not a guarantee. The Wolf’s altered warmth
must remain observable behavior rather than an announced motive.
